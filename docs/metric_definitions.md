# Metric Definitions

This document defines the key procurement metrics used in the **Procure-to-Pay Analytics Dashboard**.

The purpose of the metric dictionary is to make the dashboard calculations transparent and distinguish financial transaction metrics from purchase-order operational metrics.

---

## Executive KPIs

### Supplier Invoice Spend

**Definition:**
Total supplier invoice value excluding tax.

**Calculation logic:**
Sum of `Total Excluding Tax` for transactions classified as **Supplier Invoice**.

**Data source:**
`FactSupplierTransaction`

**Purpose:**
Measures the financial value of supplier invoices within the selected reporting period.

> This metric is based on supplier financial transactions and is not calculated from purchase-order quantities or stock-item prices.

---

### Purchase Orders

**Definition:**
Number of distinct purchase orders created within the selected period.

**Calculation logic:**
Distinct count of `WWI Purchase Order ID`.

**Data source:**
`FactPurchase`

**Purpose:**
Measures procurement transaction activity at purchase-order level.

---

### Purchase Order Lines

**Definition:**
Total number of purchase-order line records.

**Calculation logic:**
Count of rows in `FactPurchase`.

**Data source:**
`FactPurchase`

**Purpose:**
Measures the detailed purchasing workload generated by purchase orders.

A single purchase order can contain multiple PO lines.

---

### Outstanding Balance

**Definition:**
Total recorded outstanding balance associated with supplier transactions.

**Data source:**
`FactSupplierTransaction`

**Purpose:**
Provides visibility into supplier financial exposure recorded in the dataset.

> Outstanding balance is treated as a recorded transaction balance. It is **not automatically interpreted as overdue debt**, because the dataset does not provide sufficient aging information to make that assumption.

---

### Receipt Completion %

**Definition:**
Percentage of ordered outer packages that have been received.

**Calculation logic:**

`Received Outers ÷ Ordered Outers`

**Data source:**
`FactPurchase`

**Purpose:**
Measures overall fulfilment of ordered quantities.

---

## Purchase Order Operations

### Ordered Outers

**Definition:**
Total number of supplier outer-packaging units ordered.

**Calculation logic:**
Sum of `Ordered Outers`.

**Data source:**
`FactPurchase`

---

### Received Outers

**Definition:**
Total number of supplier outer-packaging units received.

**Calculation logic:**
Sum of `Received Outers`.

**Data source:**
`FactPurchase`

---

### Receipt Gap

**Definition:**
Difference between ordered and received outer quantities.

**Calculation logic:**

`Ordered Outers - Received Outers`

**Interpretation:**

* `0` = ordered quantity fully received
* Positive value = quantity remains unreceived

---

### Unfinalized PO Lines

**Definition:**
Number of purchase-order lines that are not marked as finalized.

**Calculation logic:**
Count of PO lines where `Is Order Finalized = False`.

**Purpose:**
Highlights purchase-order lines that may still require operational completion.

---

### PO Line Finalization %

**Definition:**
Share of purchase-order lines marked as finalized.

**Purpose:**
Provides a high-level view of purchasing-process completion.

---

## Supplier Transaction Metrics

### Supplier Invoice Count

**Definition:**
Number of distinct supplier invoice transactions.

**Calculation logic:**
Distinct count of `WWI Supplier Transaction ID`, filtered to transaction type **Supplier Invoice**.

**Data source:**
`FactSupplierTransaction`

**Reason for using transaction ID:**
The supplier transaction ID provides a more reliable unique transaction identifier than relying only on supplier invoice number.

---

### Average Invoice Value

**Definition:**
Average financial value of a supplier invoice excluding tax.

**Calculation logic:**

`Supplier Invoice Spend ÷ Supplier Invoice Count`

**Purpose:**
Shows the typical financial size of supplier invoices.

---

### Supplier Invoice Tax

**Definition:**
Total tax associated with supplier invoice transactions.

**Data source:**
`FactSupplierTransaction`

**Purpose:**
Provides visibility into tax value associated with procurement invoices.

---

### Supplier Invoices with Balance

**Definition:**
Number of supplier invoice transactions with a recorded outstanding balance.

**Purpose:**
Identifies invoices where a transaction balance remains recorded.

> This should not automatically be interpreted as the number of overdue invoices.

---

### Outstanding Balance as % of Invoice Spend

**Definition:**
Outstanding balance expressed as a percentage of supplier invoice spend.

**Calculation logic:**

`Outstanding Balance ÷ Supplier Invoice Spend`

**Display rule:**
The KPI reference is displayed only when **one reporting year is selected**.

**Purpose:**
Provides context for the size of the outstanding balance relative to invoice activity during the selected year.

---

## Stock Item Metrics

### Purchased Stock Items

**Definition:**
Number of distinct stock items with purchase activity.

**Business identifier:**
`WWI Stock Item ID`

**Purpose:**
Measures the breadth of the purchased item portfolio.

---

### One-Off Stock Items

**Definition:**
Stock items appearing on exactly **one distinct purchase order**.

**Calculation logic:**

`PO Count = 1`

**Purpose:**
Identifies items purchased only once within the current reporting context.

---

### Repeat-Order Stock Items

**Definition:**
Stock items appearing on more than one distinct purchase order.

**Calculation logic:**

`PO Count > 1`

**Purpose:**
Identifies items associated with recurring purchasing activity.

---

### High-Frequency Stock Items

**Definition:**
Stock items appearing on more than **100 distinct purchase orders**.

**Calculation logic:**

`PO Count > 100`

**Purpose:**
Identifies the small group of items generating particularly high recurring procurement workload.

> The 100-PO threshold is an analytical classification used for this portfolio project based on the observed distribution of the dataset. It is not presented as a universal procurement benchmark.

---

### Average Order Quantity per PO Line

**Definition:**
Average number of outer-packaging units ordered for each purchase-order line.

**Calculation logic:**

`Ordered Outers ÷ PO Line Count`

**Purpose:**
Measures typical order quantity at PO-line level.

---

## Supplier Concentration Analysis

### Cumulative PO %

**Definition:**
Running percentage of total purchase orders accounted for by suppliers ranked from highest to lowest PO count.

**Calculation concept:**

`Cumulative Purchase Orders ÷ Total Purchase Orders`

**Purpose:**
Used in the supplier Pareto analysis to identify concentration of procurement workload.

The measure retains report-level selections while calculating cumulative activity across the suppliers visible within the selected reporting context.

---

## Understanding “Outers”

The Wide World Importers dataset records procurement quantities in **outer packaging units**.

An outer represents a supplier purchasing or shipping package rather than necessarily one individual product.

For example:

* Buying package: Carton
* Quantity per outer: 12 units
* Ordered Outers: 100

This represents **100 cartons**, potentially containing 1,200 individual product units.

For this reason, dashboard quantity metrics are described using **Ordered Outers** and **Received Outers** rather than assuming they represent individual pieces.

---

## Filter Behaviour

Dashboard metrics respond to the active report filters, including:

* Year
* Supplier
* Supplier Category

Where appropriate, reference-label measures are intentionally hidden when multiple years or **All Years** are selected to avoid displaying potentially misleading period comparisons.

## Metric Ownership by Fact Table

| Metric Area                    | Primary Fact Table        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Supplier invoice spend         | `FactSupplierTransaction` |
| Supplier invoice count         | `FactSupplierTransaction` |
| Tax                            | `FactSupplierTransaction` |
| Outstanding balance            | `FactSupplierTransaction` |
| Purchase orders                | `FactPurchase`            |
| Purchase-order lines           | `FactPurchase`            |
| Ordered / received quantities  | `FactPurchase`            |
| Receipt performance            | `FactPurchase`            |
| PO finalization                | `FactPurchase`            |
| Stock-item purchasing activity | `FactPurchase`            |

The two fact tables are analyzed through shared dimensions such as `DimDate` and `DimSupplier`; they are not joined directly at transaction level.
